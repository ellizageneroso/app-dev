# *The Intern* (2015)

**Starring:** Anne Hathaway & Robert De Niro  

---

##  **Context**

*The Intern* is a **comedy-drama film** about a 70-year-old widower named **Ben Whittaker** (played by *Robert De Niro*) who becomes a senior intern at an online fashion company founded by **Jules Ostin** (*Anne Hathaway*).  

> The movie shows how two people from different generations can learn from each other, not just in work but also in life.  

---

##  **Lesson from the Movie**

1. **Age doesn’t define ability.**  
   Ben proves that even at 70, he can still work, adapt, and make a difference.  

2. **Work-life balance matters.**  
   Jules learns that success at work means nothing if you lose yourself or your loved ones.  

3. **Kindness and wisdom never go out of style.**  
   The movie reminds us that being calm, respectful, and understanding can change how people feel at work and in life.  

---

##  **My Favorite Characters**

### **Anne Hathaway as Jules Ostin**
- *A strong and independent woman.*
- She manages her own fashion company while facing stress, pressure, and personal struggles.  
- I admire how she stays graceful and learns to trust others.  

### **Robert De Niro as Ben Whittaker**
- *A wise and gentle man.*
- He becomes a mentor and friend to everyone at the company.  
- His calm presence reminds people to slow down and appreciate life.  

---

##  **Impact on a Youth Like Me**

As a 22-year-old lady, *The Intern* inspires me because:
- It shows that it’s okay to **not have everything figured out yet**.  
- It encourages me to **respect older people’s experiences** and learn from them.  
- Jules’ journey teaches me to **stay confident and passionate** even when things get hard.  

---

### **Final Thought**

> “You’re never wrong for doing the right thing.” — Ben Whittaker  

*This movie makes me believe that every stage of life has something beautiful to offer.*
